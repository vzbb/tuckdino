Quaternius browser assets included in this directory
====================================================

Animated dinosaurs:
- Apatosaurus.glb
- Parasaurolophus.glb
- Stegosaurus.glb
- Trex.glb
- Triceratops.glb
- Velociraptor.glb

Ranch and arena props:
- Backpack.glb
- Bonfire.glb
- Compass_Open.glb
- Raft.glb
- Tent.glb
- WoodenTorch_Fire.glb
- WoodLog.glb

The source FBX files came from the Quaternius Dinosaur Animated Pack
(December 2018) and Survival Pack (September 2020). The added assets were
converted to binary glTF for the web while preserving dinosaur animation clips.
