# Sunpatch Valley — Playable World Plan

## Player promise

Raise one dinosaur through care, training, exploration, and real tactical fights. Home feels safe. Wild zones feel mysterious. Every trip produces a visible story: a discovered place, useful resource, stronger bond, defeated troublemaker, or new ranch interaction.

## Core loop

1. Prepare at Sunpatch Ranch.
2. Spend finite energy on focused training.
3. Spar safely to test a build and earn trail supplies.
4. Explore a zone from the field map.
5. Choose to avoid or engage roaming threats.
6. Defeat a zone guardian for a crest, ability, or ranch upgrade.
7. Return home, celebrate, recover, and unlock the next route.

Training is useful but cannot be spammed. Each session consumes energy, each stat has a rank cap, and later gains require wins or discoveries. Exploration and fighting replenish the economy.

## Zone ladder

### 1. Sunpatch Ranch

Safe hub. Care, campfire celebrations, finite training, Pip sparring, save management.

### 2. Whispercap Grove

Intro wilderness. Bouncy mushroom pests telegraph attacks slowly. Guardian: Elder Puffcap. Reward: spore lantern ranch interaction.

### 3. Prism Glen

Crystal hazards and fast enemies. Guardian: Shardhorn. Reward: one battle shield charge.

### 4. Silverrun Stream

Slippery crossings and pack enemies. Guardian: Brookjaw. Reward: faster field recovery.

### 5. Mossback Arena

Repeatable friendly spar ladder. First win awards Meadow Crest and companion growth.

### 6. Fernwood Wilds

First dangerous open zone. Patrols, optional elites, hidden routes. Guardian: Bramblemaw.

### 7. Future frontier

Volcanic ridge, moonlit marsh, ruined observatory. Each needs its own traversal rule, enemy family, boss, and ranch consequence.

## Combat rules

- Three readable moves: power, agility, heart.
- Enemy intent shown before each turn.
- Correct counter earns momentum; repeating one move loses efficiency.
- Defeat returns player to ranch with a small resource loss, never deletes progress.
- Sparring has reduced rewards and no loss.
- Open-world enemies respawn after recovery or a new expedition.
- Bosses stay defeated in save data and permanently change world state.

## Economy

- Energy: spent training; restored by exploration, resting, or a new day.
- Trail treats: earned from sparring and discoveries; spent on advanced training.
- Momentum: temporary battle resource earned by counters.
- Crests: permanent progression keys from guardians and arena milestones.

## Content budget

- Keep 3–6 animated characters active near player.
- Reuse loaded rigs and animation clips.
- Instance repeated trees, rocks, mushrooms, and crowd props.
- One hero enemy model per zone; palette or scale variants for regular patrols.
- Load boss models only inside their zone or encounter.

## Asset targets

- Quaternius Ultimate Stylized Nature: primary terrain dressing.
- Quaternius Cute Animated Monsters: first enemy family.
- Quaternius Universal Base Characters + modular outfits: Pip, ranch residents, travelers, arena crowd, later player avatars.
- Current Quaternius dinosaur models: rivals, guardians, wildlife, and temporary enemy stand-ins.

## Sprint completion bar

First vertical slice is complete when a new save can train finitely, spar, discover zones on map, fight at least one roaming enemy, defeat one boss-like guardian, receive a persistent reward, return to a visibly changed ranch, delete the save, and repeat without console errors.
